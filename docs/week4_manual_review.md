## What changed from the original selection, and why

The original five were **C16, C17, C21, C14, C23**. After the correction:

- **C17 is out.** It's 2,076ha — above the 1,000ha Investigation Zone
  threshold — and the new protected-site check found it overlaps **five
  separate SSSIs** (Clitheroe Knoll Reefs, Coplow Quarry, Little Mearley
  Clough, Salthill and Bellmanpark Quarries) plus a listed structure. Both
  facts were invisible under the original methodology: size wasn't acted on,
  and no protected-site data existed yet. It remains rank-stable (4/4) by
  score, which is exactly why this correction mattered - a high, stable rank
  was masking a site that isn't really deliverable as a single "site card"
  at all.
- **C22 is in**, replacing it. Clean, geographically distinct from the C16/
  C21 cluster, best-ranked genuinely clean option available. Its rank-
  stability (1/4) is markedly weaker than C16/C21/C14's — worth being
  upfront about rather than presenting all five as equally robust.
- **C23 stays, but its story changed.** Previously presented as a clean
  candidate near Settle; it now carries a real protected-site flag. It's
  kept on the same logic as before (only credible option covering the upper
  catchment) but the card must now say so plainly.
